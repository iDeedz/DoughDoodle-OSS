## Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Set Your Monthly Income
1. Navigate to your dashboard
2. Find the "Monthly Income" card in the left sidebar
3. Click "Set Income" button
4. Enter your take-home pay (after taxes)
5. Click "Save"

**💡 Tip:** Use your net income (after taxes and deductions), not your gross salary.

### Step 2: Create Budget Categories (Envelopes)
DoughDoodle automatically creates default categories, but you can customize them:

**Essential Categories:**
- 🏠 Housing (Rent/Mortgage)
- 🛒 Groceries
- 🚗 Transportation
- ⚡ Utilities
- 📱 Phone/Internet
- 🏥 Healthcare

**Lifestyle Categories:**
- 🍽️ Dining Out
- 🎬 Entertainment
- 👕 Clothing
- 💰 Emergency Fund
- 🎯 Savings Goals
- 🎉 Fun Money

### Step 3: Allocate Your Budget
1. Click on any category name
2. Enter the amount you want to budget
3. Click "Set Budget"
4. Repeat for all categories

**🎯 Goal:** Your total budgeted amount should equal your monthly income.

### Step 4: Record Transactions
1. Find the appropriate category
2. Click "Add Transaction"
3. Enter description and amount
4. Choose the date
5. Click "Save"

### Step 5: Monitor and Adjust
- Check progress bars (green = good, yellow = warning, red = overspent)
- Review remaining amounts
- Transfer money between envelopes as needed
- Adjust budget amounts monthly based on actual spending

## Understanding Envelope Budgeting

### What is Envelope Budgeting?
Envelope budgeting is a time-tested method where you divide your income into different "envelopes" (categories) for specific expenses.

### Traditional Method vs. DoughDoodle

| Traditional | DoughDoodle |
|-------------|-------------|
| Physical cash in envelopes | Digital envelopes with real-time tracking |
| Manual calculations | Automatic balance updates |
| Limited visibility | Comprehensive analytics |
| No guidance | AI-powered recommendations |

### Why It Works
1. **Prevents Overspending:** You can only spend what's allocated
2. **Gives Purpose:** Every dollar has a specific job
3. **Builds Awareness:** You see exactly where money goes
4. **Encourages Saving:** Leftover money stays in envelopes

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### "Available to Budget" Shows Negative Number
**Cause:** You've allocated more money than your income
**Solution:** 
1. Reduce budget amounts in some categories
2. Or increase your monthly income if you have additional sources

#### Can't Find a Transaction
**Solutions:**
1. Use the global search bar (top navigation)
2. Go to Tools → Advanced Search for detailed filtering
3. Check if it's in a different month or category

#### Progress Bar Showing Wrong Percentage
**Cause:** Usually due to budget amount being $0
**Solution:** Set a budget amount for that category

#### Import Not Working
**Common Issues:**
1. File format not supported (use CSV)
2. Columns not mapped correctly
3. Date format not recognized

**Solutions:**
1. Export sample CSV from your bank
2. Map columns carefully during import
3. Check date format (MM/DD/YYYY preferred)

#### AI Recommendations Not Loading
**Causes:**
1. No internet connection
2. Insufficient data (need at least 2 weeks of transactions)

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Wait for more data to accumulate
3. System provides fallback recommendations

**Q: Can I change categories after setup?**
A: Yes! Add, remove, or modify categories anytime.
